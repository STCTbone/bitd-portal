# Recover participant identities with Google

The group requested Google login on 2026-10-21 after reviewing the browser-bound guest experience. Google is the primary recoverable sign-in method; guests remain available for local previews and existing campaigns. This supersedes the anonymous-only entry assumption in ADR 0002, while retaining its backend authority and confidentiality decisions. Email links would require mail delivery and passwords would add password recovery; Google provides the requested recovery across browsers without either application flow.

Link an existing guest to Google in place so its participant ID, memberships and character ownership remain stable. Do not silently replace the guest with an existing Google account or merge accounts by matching email/name: identity collisions require another Google account or explicit invitation/GM-assisted recovery. A Google identity grants no campaign role by itself. Campaign invitations and current backend memberships remain authoritative. My campaigns reads only the caller's existing RLS-protected records, and opening one revalidates membership.

Use the Supabase SDK's PKCE callback flow. Auth identity changes immediately discard the previous workspace and pending responses; signing out affects the current browser session and its tabs, not other devices. Browser persistence retains a convenient session and last campaign, but Google can recover the identity without that browser data. Google client credentials stay in backend configuration. Live Google consent/callback verification requires the operator's configured OAuth client and is separate from local account/session tests.
