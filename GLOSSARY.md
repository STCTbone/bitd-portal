# Blades ’68 Portal

Shared language for the gaming group's adaptation of the Blades in the Dark portal.

## Language

**Blades ’68**:
The published Blades in the Dark supplement by Tim Denee, set in Doskvol a century after the original game. Its published rules are the baseline for this group's portal.
_Avoid_: Using “Blades” alone when the distinction between the original game and the supplement matters.

**House rule**:
A deliberate change or addition that this gaming group makes to the published rules. House rules are identified explicitly rather than presented as published Blades ’68 rules.

**Participant identity**:
The identity a participant uses to return to their campaigns and owned characters. Signing in with the same linked Google account recovers that identity across devices.

**Campaign membership**:
A participant's access and GM or player role in one campaign. Having an account alone does not grant campaign membership.

**GM-only information**:
Campaign information entrusted to the GM until they choose to share it with the players, such as a hidden clock or private faction notes.
_Avoid_: Treating information as shared merely because a player has joined the campaign.
